How it works (and how it's measured)

1 · A StonkFun reward token

$GROYPER launched on StonkFun (Raydium LaunchLab) on Sept 11, 2026 and moved to a Raydium CPMM GROYPER/SOL pool. The mint is a Token-2022 token with a 3% transfer fee (300 bps), so every buy, sell and transfer leaves 3% behind as withheld GROYPER.

2 · From tax to SOL payouts

StonkFun's reward wallet 5KXD…K6tD (the mint's fee authority) harvests the withheld GROYPER, swaps it to SOL and sends wrapped SOL to holders pro rata to their balance, every 1–3 days in batches of ~15 wallets per transaction. Pools are excluded; recent payouts suggest a minimum of about 100,000 GROYPER (smallest recent recipient).

3 · What this site reads

The payout wallets are shared with other StonkFun reward tokens, so a transfer alone doesn't say which token it was for. Attribution comes from StonkFun's public payout ledger for this mint; then every ledger transaction is re-read on-chain (Helius) and the holder's wSOL balance change must match the ledger amount. Results are built on a server into this site's data files.

4 · Limits, honestly

Payout history is a snapshot: payouts after the build time show up at the next refresh. USD values use today's SOL price. The eligibility tag is an estimate from your current balance. Forecasts extrapolate past payout rates and are not a promise of anything.
